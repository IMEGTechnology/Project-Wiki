# Contributing

> [!NOTE] About this guide
> This guide is for Contributors and Administrators. It covers the jobs you have on top of everything in [Using Folio](help.md): reviewing pages, answering comments, starting a new page and looking at the templates. Writing a page is a separate guide, [Editing in Obsidian](help-edit.md). Written for **Version 0.85.0**.

Most of what you do here starts from **Admin** on Home. Review, All comments and Templates are three of its doors. **New page** is in the header. **New here? Do Find what needs your review and Approve or flag a page.** That is the heart of the job.

## Find what needs your review
%% card: The list of pages waiting for someone to look at them | icon: check | updated: 0.83 | key: review %%

Review is the list of pages that are waiting for a person. On Home, open **Admin**, then **Review**. The numbers match the picture.

![[co-review.png|594]]
*Review: one row per page, oldest first.*

1. **All**, **To be reviewed**, **Flagged** and **Questions** narrow the list to one kind.
2. The count and the coloured bar say how many pages wait, and why.
3. **Start review** walks you through the pages, oldest first.
4. **Review page** opens one page.

> [!tip]
> A page is one row, however much it owes. The row says everything the page needs, so you deal with it in one visit.

> [!note]- More detail
> **What lands here.** A new page made with New page. A page someone changed, in Obsidian or in Folio, since it was last approved. A page someone flagged, or reported with the **Report** button at its foot. A question or escalation in its comments. A page whose due date has passed.
>
> **How Folio notices a change.** Once a page's text is new or different and has sat for two minutes, it is marked **To be reviewed**. A page that was only renamed or moved keeps its sign-off.
>
> **When a row leaves.** When the page owes nothing any more. Approving it, or closing its last open comment, takes it off the list.
>
> **Oldest first.** A page with no readable date goes to the bottom, because Folio cannot tell how long it has waited.
>
> **Missing properties** are not here. An Administrator finds them in Vault check.

## Approve or flag a page
%% card: Look at what changed, then sign it off or send it back | icon: check | updated: 0.83 | key: approve %%

Open a page from Review and Folio marks what changed, with the review bar along the bottom. The numbers match the picture.

![[co-approve.png|760]]
*Reviewing a page: what changed is marked, and the review bar sits along the bottom.*

1. A changed or new section is marked in the page. Deleted text can be shown too.
2. **prev** and **next** step through the changes.
3. **Approve page** signs the page off.
4. **Flag** asks what needs looking at and posts your answer as a comment on the page.
5. **Next page** goes on to the one after it.

> [!tip]
> Approving says you looked at the changes. It does not say every fact was checked.

> [!note]- More detail
> **The colours.** Green **NEW SECTION**: the heading was not there last time. Amber **CHANGED**: that section's words are different. Red dashed **REMOVED**: the section is gone. Folio draws it back in so you can see what went. It is not in the file.
>
> **What approving does.** It signs the page off: the status becomes Reviewed and today's date goes in the page's reviewed property. It never changes the words. It is recorded under your name, and anyone at Contributor can approve anything, including their own edits.
>
> **Flag.** The page stays on the Review list as Flagged until its comment is closed. The comment carries your name. Flag does not undo anything, because a change made in Obsidian has already reached everyone and someone may have the page open.
>
> **Show deleted.** Tick it to see the removed text, marked in the page.
>
> **Version history.** OneDrive keeps it, not Folio. To see who changed a file, or to put an old version back, right-click the file in your synced vault folder and choose **Version history**.
>
> **A new page.** If it still has the grey notes or guide boxes from its template, Folio asks whether to remove them. **OK** removes them and then approves. **Cancel** approves and keeps them. Folio hides both while people read.
>
> **Properties on the bar.** For a new page, a page due to be read again, or one set to be reviewed, the bar shows the page's properties. Fix what is wrong and press **Save properties**. It writes only what you changed. Then press **Approve**.
>
> **If it was edited in Folio.** Only Administrators can do that. The bar then shows **Accept** and **Reject** for each change, and **Accept all** when there are several. **Reject puts the page back as it was before that edit**, and drops any later changes waiting behind it. If you only want to ask a question about an edit, leave a comment instead.
>
> **Leaving.** The **X** at the right of the bar, **Dashboard**, Home, or opening any other page ends the walk. Your place is not kept.

## Answer comments
%% card: Every open comment in the vault, in one place | icon: comment | updated: 0.83 | key: comments %%

All comments gathers every open comment in the vault, grouped by page. On Home, open **Admin**, then **All comments**. The numbers match the picture.

![[co-all-comments.png|618]]
*All comments: every open comment in the vault, grouped by page.*

1. The count says how many comments are open, and the bar says what kind.
2. **Open**, **Closed** or **All**.
3. **Open** takes you to the page at that comment, so you can reply where it was left.
4. **Mark closed** once it is dealt with.

> [!tip]
> A closed comment is not deleted. Choose **Closed** or **All** to see it again.

> [!note]- More detail
> **A question or escalation on the review bar.** When a walk reaches one, the bar shows the whole thread. Type in the box and press **Post reply**. **Jump to section** shows the part of the page it is about. **Assign / due…** lets you type a name and a due date. **Close** closes it.
>
> **Assign is a typed name.** Folio has no list of people to pick from, so a name that matches nobody is still saved.
>
> **Kinds.** The bar splits the open comments into flags and escalations, questions, and notes and actions.
>
> **Writing a comment** is in [Using Folio](help.md).

## Start a new page
%% card: Pick a template, give it a title, and open it in Obsidian | icon: file | updated: 0.83 | key: new-page %%

New page sets up a page from a template, with its number already chosen, and opens it in Obsidian so you can write. Click **New page** in the header. The numbers match the picture.

![[ad-new-page.png|760]]
*New page: Folio sets it up in Working Drafts and opens it in Obsidian.*

1. Pick the **template**. The box under it says what it is for, and what it is not for.
2. Type the **title**.
3. Choose the folder it **will be filed in**.
4. The **number** is chosen for you.
5. Click **Create and open in Obsidian**.

> [!tip]
> The list on the right shows the headings the page starts with, and which are Core, Repeat, One of, or Optional.

> [!note]- More detail
> **Where the page goes.** Into the Working Drafts folder, as a draft. It waits there until someone files it in the folder you chose. Drafts say so at the top of the page.
>
> **Numbers.** Always three digits, counted separately in each top folder. Folio never renames a page for you.
>
> **Grey notes and guide boxes.** They tell you what goes under each heading. Folio keeps them in the file but hides them for readers. Delete them as you write, or let Approve offer to remove them.
>
> **Obsidian.** Folio opens the page with a link, so Obsidian must have this vault open under the same name. If it does not open, see Something is wrong.
>
> **Writing it.** [Editing in Obsidian](help-edit.md) covers properties, pictures, tables and callouts.

## Look at the templates
%% card: See each page type's shape, its rules, and the AI prompts | icon: template | updated: 0.83 | key: templates %%

Templates are the starting shape for each kind of page. On Home, open **Admin**, then **Templates**. The numbers match the picture.

![[ad-templates.png|760]]
*Templates: the starting shape for each kind of page.*

1. One tab for each template, with how many pages use it.
2. **Sample** shows the page, **Outline** lists its headings, **AI prompts** copies a ready-made request for your AI.
3. **+ New** adds a template. Administrators only.
4. **Format rules** are the rules every page is checked against.

> [!tip]
> The AI prompts copy together with the template and the Format rules, so what your AI writes follows the same rules Folio checks.

> [!note]- More detail
> **The four prompts.** **Write a page** turns your notes into a new page. **Fix a page** moves an older page into the template without changing what it says. **Check a page** lists what breaks the rules without rewriting it. **Update this template** is for Administrators changing a template.
>
> **View.** The button at the right of the Sample switches between **In Folio**, **In Obsidian**, the raw **File**, and **As a new page**.
>
> **Contributors read, Administrators change.** Adding, editing or retiring a template, and changing a Format rule, are Administrator jobs. A retired template is no longer offered in New page, and pages already written with it do not change.
>
> **Templates are edited in Obsidian.** Folio only shows them.

## Something is wrong
%% card: Fixes by what you see | icon: alert | updated: 0.83 | key: trouble %%

Find what you see below and open it. Each one says why it happens, then what to do.

### Folio says it could not approve, save or close
Cause: Folio could not write to the vault. Usually the folder is view only for you, or OneDrive is paused.

Fix: check that OneDrive is running and that you can edit files in the folder. If you can, tell your Administrator.

### Create and open in Obsidian did not open Obsidian
Cause: Obsidian does not have this vault added under the same name Folio uses.

Fix: open Obsidian once, choose **Open folder as vault**, and pick the vault folder. Then open the page from Working Drafts. The page was already created.

### The page I just approved is still on the list
Cause: the page still owes something else, such as an open comment or a due date.

Fix: open the page. Its row says what it still owes.
